Who We Are

Care that respects, comforts, and supports

Golden Olive Companion Care Limited provides reliable, person-centred support designed to help individuals live comfortably and independently at home. Founded by an experienced NHS nursing professional, our service is built on clinical knowledge, compassion, and a deep understanding of individual care needs.

Our focus is on dignity, compassion, and enhancing everyday quality of life — giving families peace of mind and loved ones the support they deserve.
